# Economics Paper Writing

## Core Rule

Put the central contribution first. Do not make the reader wait for the main result, identification argument, or behavioral mechanism.

Use this order at the paper level, section level, paragraph level, and sentence level:

1. State what the paper does or finds.
2. Explain the mechanism, evidence, or method.
3. Compare to alternatives or prior literature.
4. Defend against concerns and robustness checks.

## Behavioral Economics Emphasis

- Make the behavioral mechanism concrete.
- Distinguish the psychological mechanism from the empirical pattern.
- Avoid presenting a behavioral explanation as the only possible explanation unless the design rules out alternatives.
- Explain what behavior is being modeled: choices, beliefs, attention, learning, updating, self-control, social preferences, or strategic responses.
- Connect the empirical specification to the behavioral hypothesis.
- Report economic magnitudes, not only statistical significance.

## Abstract

- Keep it short.
- State the central contribution and main finding.
- Say what the paper finds, not merely what it studies.
- Avoid literature review in the abstract.
- Include the design or data only if needed to understand the result.

## Introduction

- Start with the contribution, not a broad motivation.
- Give the concrete fact behind the result.
- Keep the introduction focused and short.
- Add literature only after the reader understands the paper's contribution.
- Avoid generic openings such as "Economists have long been interested in..."

## Literature Review

- Focus on the closest papers.
- Be generous and accurate.
- Explain how this paper differs after explaining what this paper does.
- Avoid turning the literature review into a full field survey unless the paper itself requires it.

## Empirical Sections

- Get to the main result quickly.
- Put only necessary material before the main result.
- Explain the source of identifying variation.
- Explain the error term in economic terms.
- Explain why the identifying variation is plausibly unrelated to the error term.
- Use controls and instruments deliberately; do not add variables only to raise fit.
- Show stylized facts that make the result intuitive.

## Tables And Figures

- Give each table and figure a self-contained caption.
- Discuss every table or figure in the text.
- Remove numbers that are not interpreted.
- Use sensible units and rounded values.
- Label axes and define symbols.
- Prefer figures when they reveal the pattern more clearly than tables.

## Prose Style

- Use active voice.
- Prefer present tense.
- Use simple subject-verb-object sentences.
- Use concrete nouns and verbs.
- Remove throat-clearing phrases.
- Avoid inflated adjectives for the author's own results.
- Avoid repetition, unnecessary footnotes, and roadmap paragraphs unless they genuinely help.
