# Introduction Formula

A paragraph-by-paragraph template for an economics paper introduction. Keep the whole thing short (often under two pages).

1. **Hook.** The concrete question and why it matters. At most two sentences of motivation — no "Economists have long been interested in...".
2. **What this paper does.** The setting, design, and data in plain words. What variation is used.
3. **Main findings.** State the results with economic magnitudes, not just signs and significance.
4. **Mechanism / interpretation.** Why the result arises; the behavioral or economic channel.
5. **Contribution.** How this differs from the 3–5 closest papers. Be generous and accurate; position, don't survey.
6. **Roadmap (optional).** Include only if the structure is nonstandard.

Rules:
- The reader should know the main result by the end of the first page.
- Give the concrete fact behind the result early.
- Every claim here is later delivered in the body — no promises the paper does not keep.
