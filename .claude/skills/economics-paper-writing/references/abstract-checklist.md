# Abstract Checklist

- Target ≤ 150 words.
- The first sentence states what the paper finds, not what it studies.
- Include the design and data only as far as needed to understand the result.
- Report the main finding with a magnitude where possible.
- End with the contribution or the implication.
- No citations. No "we discuss" / "this paper explores" throat-clearing.
- No literature review.
