# Results Section

- Lead with the main result. Do not make the reader wade through secondary analyses first.
- One table or figure per claim. Tie every exhibit to a sentence of interpretation.
- Interpret magnitude before significance: what is the effect in percent, or relative to the control mean, and does it matter economically?
- Do not narrate tables cell by cell. Report the numbers that carry the argument; leave the rest to the exhibit.
- Compress robustness into one subsection or an appendix pointer; do not let it crowd out the main finding.
- State the identifying assumption near the main estimate and the evidence that supports it.
- Every number stated in prose must exist in a generated output — never typed by hand.
